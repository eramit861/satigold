FAQ

Questions we often hear

Is Sati Gold honey raw and unfiltered?

Yes. Sati Gold is raw, unfiltered honey collected from wild Himalayan flowers in Uttarakhand. We do not add sugar, syrups, colours or preservatives.

Where does Sati Gold honey come from?

Our honey comes from Village Tarari in District Almora, Uttarakhand, where bees forage on wild Himalayan flowers in the clean mountain valleys.

Why has my honey thickened or crystallised?

Crystallisation is a natural sign of pure, raw honey, especially in cold weather. Place the closed jar in warm (not boiling) water for a few minutes and it will turn liquid again.

What is the shelf life of the honey?

Best before 24 months from the date of packing. Store the jar tightly closed in a cool, dry place and always use a dry spoon.
